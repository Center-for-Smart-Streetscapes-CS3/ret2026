Center for Smart Streetscapes / Columbia Engineering

Research Experience for Teachers at Columbia University

CS3 RET is a four-week summer program for New York City high school teachers. Participants study machine learning, computer vision, and urban sensing and develop classroom projects.

View the curriculum About the program

Program format

Teachers work with CS3 researchers at Columbia.

Teachers join Columbia faculty and graduate researchers for projects in computer vision and urban sensing. Sessions cover the methods, datasets, and tools used in CS3 research.

Teachers use this work to prepare lessons and a final project for their students.

Course structure

Four weeks of instruction and project work.

Full calendar

Week 1

Introduction: From Machine Learning to Agentic AI

Neural networks, circuits, machine learning, computer vision, agentic workflows, and responsible AI.

Week 2

Experiments for Students

Object tracking, language models, and object detection with NYC data.

Week 3

Various Topics on Vision and AI

Depth estimation, model evaluation, and edge deployment.

Week 4

Advanced Topics and Presentation Prep

AI demonstrations, lesson plan work, and final presentations.
