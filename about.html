About the program

Research Experience for Teachers at Columbia University

The CS3 RET program brings New York City high school STEM teachers to Columbia University for four weeks of research and curriculum development.

Program overview

The Center for Smart Streetscapes (CS3) is a National Science Foundation Engineering Research Center led by Columbia University. RET participants work with CS3 faculty, staff, and graduate researchers.

The program covers machine learning, computer vision, and urban sensing. Teachers attend lectures, labs, workshops, and research sessions.

Each teacher prepares a classroom module and a final project using New York City data.

The 2026 program runs July 13 through August 6, 2026, at Morningside Heights Campus, Mudd Building, Room 706.

Columbia Engineering administers CS3 RET with support from the Center for Smart Streetscapes and the National Science Foundation.
